# Invest in Dubai Property

**Zenith Group website development · Investor education and offers navigation**

[Open the live site](https://investindubaiproperty.net/) · [Evidence and verification](../../evidence/README.md)

## Business context

| Audience | Website goal |
| --- | --- |
| Visitors researching property investment in Dubai and those ready to browse opportunities. | Create a journey from introductory content to a deeper guide, current offers, and an enquiry option. |

## My contribution

I developed this website as part of Zenith Group’s web development program. I use this site to demonstrate information architecture for different stages of research. The guide serves visitors who need background, while offers give visitors a shorter route to browsing opportunities.

## What you can inspect

The homepage groups investment topics and links to both a dedicated property investment guide and a current-offers page. It also includes a consultation path.

1. [Start at the homepage](https://investindubaiproperty.net/).
2. [Read the property investment guide](https://investindubaiproperty.net/dubai-property-investment/).
3. [Browse current offers](https://investindubaiproperty.net/current-offers/).

**Review question:** Can a visitor move from a topic overview into an explanatory page and from there find the offers route through the primary navigation?
