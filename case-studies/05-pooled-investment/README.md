## Business context

| Audience | Website goal |
| --- | --- |
| Visitors exploring how pooled property investment works and looking for a next step. | Give visitors a dedicated educational route, an offers destination, and an enquiry path for a specific investment topic. |

## My contribution

I developed this website as part of Zenith Group’s web development program. I use this site to demonstrate a topic-specific website within a wider estate. Keeping the explanation and offers on separate pages lets visitors choose between learning the model and seeing available material.

## What you can inspect

The homepage links to a pooled-investment explainer and a current-offers page. The site uses separate navigation for education, offers, and company information, with an enquiry link on the homepage.

1. [Start at the homepage](https://dubaipooledinvestment.com/).
2. [Open the pooled-investment guide](https://dubaipooledinvestment.com/what-is-pool-investment/).
3. [Browse pooled-investment offers](https://dubaipooledinvestment.com/dubai-pooled-investment-offers/).

**Review question:** Can visitors distinguish the educational guide from the offers page and find both through the public navigation?
